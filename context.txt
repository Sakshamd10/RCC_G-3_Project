what do u mean here by generations and designs

This is one of the most important concepts in NSGA-II. In your project, a Design is one complete version of your G+3 building, while a Generation is one round of optimization in which NSGA-II evaluates a group of designs and then creates improved ones for the next round.

Think of it like a class test.

Design = One student's answer sheet.

Generation = One whole class taking the test.

After checking all answer sheets, the teacher picks the best students and asks them to create improved answers for the next test. That next test is the next generation.

In your project report

You can describe it like this:

Design: One complete set of structural dimensions (beam size, column size, concrete grade, etc.) for the G+3 building.

Population: The number of designs evaluated together in one optimization cycle (e.g., 20 designs).

Generation: One optimization cycle in which NSGA-II evaluates the entire population, selects the best-performing designs, and creates a new population for the next cycle through crossover and mutation.

This terminology is exactly how NSGA-II is described in research papers, but here each "individual" in the algorithm corresponds to one complete STAAD building model.
